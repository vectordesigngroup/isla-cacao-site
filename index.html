Nuestra historia

A wild peninsula, a family, and a bean worth the trip.

Macanao Cacao is a family venture between a son in Gainesville, Florida and his father on Margarita Island, Venezuela. Macanao is the island's wild, arid western peninsula: cactus, dry hills and quiet beaches facing the Caribbean. The name is a nod to my dad's home on Margarita, just across the water from the cacao coast of Paria.

On the ground in Venezuela, my father leads supplier vetting and quality. He's a retired accountant and former audit partner, and he brings an auditor's eye to every lot: who grew it, how it was fermented and dried, and whether the paperwork holds up.

In Florida, I handle importing, partners and, soon, our own chocolate. Padre e hijo, isla y continente.

Origen

Why Venezuelan cacao

Venezuela is home to some of the world's most celebrated cacao, with a deep heritage of Criollo and Criollo-descended trees. The International Cocoa Organization (ICCO) lists Venezuela among its fine or flavour cocoa origins.

Sucre

Paria & Río Caribe

The cacao coast closest to Margarita: green mountains running down to the Caribbean. Our starting point.

Miranda

Barlovento

Home of Carenero Superior, one of Venezuela's named fine-flavor cacao origins.

Zulia

Sur del Lago

The southwest lowlands south of Lake Maracaibo, a region known for Criollo-type cacao such as Porcelana.

Para chocolateros

For craft chocolate makers

We're building a small, careful supply of fine-flavor Venezuelan beans for US bean-to-bar makers who want traceable lots without the guesswork.

  • Small lots. Sized for craft makers, not commodity buyers.
  • Traceable origin. We only name an origin when the lot's paper trail supports it.
  • An auditor on the ground. Supplier vetting, cut tests and documentation checked in Venezuela before anything ships.
  • Compliance first. US import requirements and sanctions screening reviewed before any lot moves.

We're lining up our first lots now. Tell us what origins, volumes and flavor profiles you're looking for, and we'll reach out when samples are available.
